Quality & Analytics

Trilogy operates QA as a 360° approach. We focus first and foremost on downstream (agent-controllable) insights, where an agent can improve, where the operation can improve, and, most importantly, the segmentation of initiatives into quick wins and long-term plans, so we can implement meaningful “low-hanging fruit” improvements alongside strategic initiatives across the tenure of the contract. Our analytics engine also surfaces upstream (non-agent-controllable) insights, giving you valuable information to manage your supply chain and internal operations more effectively.

Our QA runs on two parallel tracks. Automated QA (via Genii) evaluates up to 100% of interactions for sentiment, compliance and distress flags, replacing the traditional 1% manual sample. Human-led QA then evaluates a structured sample against a multi-dimension scorecard, with weekly and monthly calibration consistently achieving >85% evaluator agreement. Genii's II-QA scorecard is fully customisable to your KPIs and separates what an agent controls from what your business needs to fix.

II-QA Scoring Methodology

Unlike the standard two-tier weighted scorecard used by most contact centres, Genii's II-QA (Interaction Intelligence – Quality Assurance) is a fully customisable, multi-tier scorecard built around your KPIs — scoring both agent behaviour and business-level limitations. Scores are calculated as Applied Weight ÷ Applicable Weight × 100, with N/A answers excluded from the denominator so results reflect real, controllable performance.

ElementApproach
Scorecard modelBlank, fully customisable campaign built from your KPIs — multiple sections, multiple output-metric questions
Assessment / pass targetRecommended 80% assessment target and 80% pass target, tuned per programme
Scoring failuresAgent-controllable fail scores 0; non-controllable business fail scores 0 but does not affect the agent
CalibrationQuality-Manager-run gauge assessments on live calls (zero agent impact); high-variance assessors flagged

Our analytics partner Genii moves quality beyond traditional QA toward true Interaction Intelligence — turning contact-centre data from a cost centre into a strategic asset. The Four Layers of Interaction Intelligence

  • Descriptive — what happened: interaction summaries and metrics across 100% of contacts.
  • Diagnostic — why it happened: root-cause and trend analysis behind the numbers.
  • Predictive — what happens next: forecasting churn, buying signals and repeat demand before they surface.
  • Prescriptive — what to do about it: recommending the specific action or coaching path to change the outcome.
Most tools stop at the first one or two layers. Genii operates across all four — turning raw interaction data into prescriptive, revenue-driving action.

Proving Value Fast — The Discover Audit

  • Insights-as-a-Service — a Discover report in weeks, not the 6–12 months typical of enterprise platforms, proving ROI before any large commitment.
  • Four pain points solved — service (process friction and repeat demand), sales (buying signals and lead leakage), retention (early churn signals), and collections (commitment-integrity analysis) — all within compliance guardrails.
  • Human-in-the-loop Academy — upskills Quality Assessors into Interaction Intelligence Analysts, so insights are genuinely acted upon rather than shelved.
Where AI stops, Genii continues — not just signals, but solutions. Combining deep contextual analysis with operational expertise, every interaction becomes one of your most valuable assets: “we help you read between the lines to drive ROI.”

Continuous Improvement Initiatives

Continuous improvement is structured around four pillars, each directly applicable to a digital-first, asynchronous voucher operation. Improvement is data-led and time-boxed, Voice of Customer signals are operationalised within 48 hours rather than waiting for a monthly report.

  • First Contact / First Response Resolution — deep root-cause analytics identify repeat-contact drivers and digital deflection opportunities across email, WhatsApp and social.
  • CSAT / Sentiment — AI sentiment analysis across interactions with integrated Voice of Customer loops operationalised within 48 hours.
  • Agent performance improvements — automated QA on all interactions highlights areas for improvement for every agent.
  • Operational improvement — daily briefs, weekly coaching huddles and monthly MBR action tracking driven by live data, not lagging reports; leverages data to spot opportunities for ongoing agent development and improvement in operational metrics.
  • Technological improvement — the Test Kitchen designs, tests and deploys new automation in a sandbox without disrupting your live service.
  • Real-time AI insights at the touch of a button — our hr.guru, included in our QContact suite, mines real-time insights and turns queries into meaningful outcomes, enabling team leaders to identify root-cause DSAT drivers and change behaviour on the fly.

For your asynchronous channels this is particularly powerful, because tone, accuracy and completeness of written responses are the primary CX levers.
