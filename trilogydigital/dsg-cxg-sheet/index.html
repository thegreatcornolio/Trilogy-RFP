DSG insight

Embedded MVNOs win — SIMs that sit inside a bank, retail or digital relationship outperform standalone “mini-telcos.” Brands own the relationship & data; MVNE owns plumbing & compliance. CX must resolve airtime + account in one interaction.

The model that actually works in SA

Standalone MVNO play underperforms. Winners wire the SIM into an existing account — banking, retail loyalty or digital — so connectivity deepens LTV, not just ARPU.

  • Benchmark vs Capitec / FNB Connect — not vs Vodacom on price
  • Usage data must land in the brand CDP, not a telco silo
  • Rebranding airtime ≠ strategy; parent-product job does
