Quick terms

CEO glossary — only what matters for this brief
MVNO

Mobile Virtual Network Operator — a brand that sells mobile SIMs/eSIMs using another operator’s radio network. Owns the customer; doesn’t build towers.

What we offer

Trilogy Digital delivers fibre/ISP & FNO CX (activations, billing, tech, outages), MVNO launch & ops, plus digital/AI contact-centre at UK+SA scale.

FNO / ISP

FNO = Fibre Network Operator (owns the fibre). ISP = Internet Service Provider (sells the package to the end user — often via FNO wholesale).

Thesis

Embedded MVNOs win — SIMs inside a bank, retail or digital relationship outperform standalone “mini-telcos.” Same logic as fibre wholesale: the brand owns the relationship & data; the enablement partner owns plumbing, compliance and CX at scale.

Embedded MVNOs

The model that actually works

Standalone MVNO play underperforms. Winners wire the SIM into an existing account — banking, retail loyalty or digital — so connectivity deepens LTV, not just ARPU.

  • Benchmark vs bank/retail embeds — not vs MNO on price
  • Usage data must land in the brand CDP, not a telco silo
  • Parent-product job > rebranding airtime
