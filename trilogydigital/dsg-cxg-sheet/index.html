Case study · ISP / Billing CX

Webafrica — On-demand billing support at scale

National ISP billing cycles drive anxiety, volume spikes, and zero tolerance for error.

Problem

  • Needed rapid scale of billing-skilled chat agents
  • Multiple processes: VR, debit orders, suspensions, premium extractions
  • Extended / staggered hours with day-by-day flex
  • Failure = churn, reputation risk, billing escalations

Solution

  • Fully managed, on-demand chat workforce built for billing ops
  • Real-time WFM + intraday optimisation
  • Centralised QA, escalation handling, live performance monitoring
  • Aligned to Webafrica billing cycles & systems (Fibre, LTE)

Impact

  • Protected CSAT during peak billing stress
  • Fewer billing escalations & repeat contacts
  • Faster response to payment failures / suspensions
  • Accurate, compliant billing communication at scale
