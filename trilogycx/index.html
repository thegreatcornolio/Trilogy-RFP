Trilogy CX (Pty) Ltd is an offshore Business Process Outsourcing company in South Africa, helping UK and USA companies augment their customer experience while cutting the cost of customer service and sales.

Trilogy is led by seasoned BPO operators with 25+ years of combined leadership. Between them they have built and managed 30+ contact centres globally, launched 1,000-seat operations, and stood up fully operational sites in under six weeks. They know what it takes to get customer experience right, first time.

The combined leadership team brings over 25 years of industry expertise, having previously established and successfully exited two major BPOs. Over their careers they have managed more than 36 contact centre operations, scaled over 10,000 seats globally, and delivered CX programmes for brands including John Lewis & Partners, British Gas, Vodafone UK, Aldi UK, and Virgin.

Today Trilogy CX delivers through three connected offerings — outsourced BPO, GCC design-build-transfer, and AI-enabled digital CX — from South Africa for UK, US and global brands.

Why Cape Town

South Africa — and Cape Town in particular — is one of the world's premier destinations for UK-facing customer experience delivery. It combines deep English-language and UK cultural alignment, a mature BPO ecosystem, favourable time zones and compelling cost advantages, all without the quality trade-offs associated with other offshore markets.

Why the Location Wins

  • BPO ecosystem leadership — Cape Town has led South Africa’s BPO export performance for nine consecutive years, drawing on a 67,000-strong regional BPO workforce with established English-language and UK-cultural alignment.
  • Operational readiness — sites are live today; no site setup phase, landlord negotiation or infrastructure lead time.
  • ESG alignment — our flagship site holds a 5-Star Green Energy rating from the Green Building Council South Africa (GBCSA).
  • Premium infrastructure — multi-redundant fibre, N+1 generator and UPS power resilience, and biometric access control.
  • Multi-site resilience — the network spans Cape Town and Johannesburg, eliminating single geographic points of failure and enabling live volume routing between sites.
  • Time-zone synergy — GMT+2 offers just a 1–2 hour difference with the UK and extended coverage for USA programmes.

Sights in and around Cape Town

Cape Town’s lifestyle and natural beauty are a genuine magnet for talent — Table Mountain, the Atlantic seaboard, the Winelands and the Cape Peninsula all sit within easy reach of our sites.
