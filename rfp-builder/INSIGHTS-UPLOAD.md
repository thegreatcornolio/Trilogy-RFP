# Client insights documents

Used by the RFP Builder section **Why we want to work with you** (`section-21`).

## How to upload (per client)

1. Open the [RFP Builder](../index.html).
2. Add **Why we want to work with you** from the section library (usually after Executive Summary).
3. In **Editable placeholders**, click **Upload insights document**.
4. Choose the client file (**PDF, DOCX, TXT or Markdown**).
5. Review / edit the two scraped boxes, then Preview / Final commit.

Each new upload re-scrapes and overwrites those two boxes (you can still edit afterwards). Output is polished into **up to 5 clear English bullets** per box.

## Prospect Intelligence Briefs (recommended)

For Trilogy **Prospect Intelligence Brief** decks, the scraper maps:

| Brief section | Proposal box |
|---|---|
| **Key takeaways for the pitch** + **Strategic fit** | Why we want to work with you |
| **Current operating setup** + **Competitive context** (incumbent gaps / objections) | Your potential pain points |

No special renaming needed — upload the brief as-is.

## Generic documents

If it is not a Prospect Intelligence Brief, use clear headings:

```text
Why we want to work with you
- Reason one
- Reason two

Your potential pain points
- Pain point one
- Pain point two
```

Also recognised: headings containing `pain points`, `potential challenges`, `why we want to work`, `why Trilogy`, `reasons we want`, `strategic fit`, `key takeaways`, `competitive context`, `current operating setup`.
