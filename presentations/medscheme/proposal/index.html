01 — Outline & offer

What Trilogy BPO is offering

Two complementary workstreams. Sales Enablement opens UK and US demand for Medscheme’s healthcare BPO. Shared Services Gate 1 readies the operating engine behind that growth.

Workstream A · USD

Sales Enablement Solution

Trilogy BPO, working with Precision Point, will position Medscheme International in the UK and US healthcare BPO market.

  • Months 1–3: initiation and market positioning.
  • Months 4–12: market engagement and sales.
  • Cape Town lead-generation team throughout year 1.
  • Travel, expenses and marketing billed pass-through.
  • 15% commission on revenues Medscheme bills directly to clients, to be validated after the first three months’ market-pricing work with Precision Point.
Workstream B · ZAR

Shared Services project — Gate 1

A three-month evaluation of Medscheme Shared Services against international BPO best practice, so the function can support international commercial models.

  • In scope: Discovery, Evaluation, Measurement.
  • Out of scope: Improvement — a later, separately agreed phase.
  • Disciplines: Recruitment, Training, Onboarding, QA, Workforce Management, AI Insights & Analytics.
  • Outcome: a roadmap with measurable outcomes and associated costs.

03 — Shared Services project

Commercials in ZAR

Gate 1 is a three-month engagement to evaluate Medscheme Shared Services and produce a roadmap. Contracting currency is South African Rand. Improvement is not included.

Gate 1 — in this proposal

1. Discovery

Trilogy BPO practitioners embed in the Medscheme environment to observe and document how each Hub discipline operates today.

›

2. Evaluation

Each discipline is assessed against international best practice, process maturity and outcomes.

›

3. Measurement

Findings are benchmarked against Trilogy BPO standards, surfacing gaps and opportunities.

›

Improvement

Not in this engagement. Follows only once Gate 1 is reviewed and a next step is agreed.

Gate 1 is designed to identify opportunities to reduce overheads through streamlining; gear Shared Services to global BPO best practice for international business; and build a capability that can support international commercial BPO models.
