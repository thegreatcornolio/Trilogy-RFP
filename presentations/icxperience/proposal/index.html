06 — GCC case studies

Design · Build · Innovate · Transfer

Two programmes that show how Trilogy stands up and scales capability centres in South Africa — the same operating DNA behind this managed-office offer.

Case study — Aegis South Africa (now Startek)

A global BPO needed a team to land in South Africa. Trilogy designed, built and operated Aegis South Africa in two geographic locations, ran the contact centre for three years, and grew the SA business by 2,500 seats.

  • Grew to 2,500 seats within 5 years, with EBITDA of over 15%.
  • Differentiation: SMART people and SMART technology.
  • Brands included British Gas, Virgin Mobile, AIG, the National Lottery, Department of Home Affairs and Telkom Mobile.
  • The operation continues in Africa as Startek, still retaining key staff Trilogy brought in to manage the business.

Case study — Ascensos

Trilogy’s DBIT team assisted Digital Tribe and Ascensos to establish a capability centre in Cape Town, with the first 100 FTEs going live within six weeks. The team designed and built a centre of excellence, oversaw and ran the operation for two to four years, and handed over a world-class operation to the incumbent.

  • Built 1,500 seats over 3 years across 4 locations.
  • Delivered over $6 million in refunds from structuring and administering government incentives.
  • Built a leadership team that remains in place and runs Ascensos South Africa.
  • Won multiple contact-centre performance awards and nationwide recognition as a top place to work.

07 — Key terms

Occupation in brief

The commercial schedule in this proposal is the face of the Trilogy CX Master Services Agreement dated 5 October 2026. The points below are the terms that affect price, notice and use.

  • Payment of rental. Paid in full, without set-off, at least five business days before each payment interval.
  • Escalation. Rental and other charges increase 7% each year, linked to Redefine, with at least 30 days’ notice, effective on the anniversary of 1 March 2027.
  • Variable charges. Parking may change from time to time and is invoiced monthly in arrears, due within 30 days of invoice.
  • Late payment. Interest at Prime plus 2%; access may be suspended; the agreement may be terminated if unpaid ten business days after written notice.
  • Notice. The agreement is for 24 months. Either party may terminate for no cause on three calendar months’ written notice, not to be given before month 12. That means the minimum commitment is 15 months, and only once the 75-seat Minimum Commitment has been fulfilled.
  • Hours. Cleaning and security are provided in the building and surrounds. Should you wish to operate 24/7, the estimate for this service is £5,000 excl. VAT per month, optional and only if elected.
  • Back office. Trilogy and iCXperience have agreed £5,000 excl. VAT per month as an initial price for Trilogy to provide back office support to iCXperience in the form of HR, payroll, facilities and management support. The parties have agreed to review this need and increase or decrease the monthly price based on iCXperience’s needs, which Trilogy will map month on month.
  • Parking is excluded from rental: 20 bays at R1,637.10 excl. VAT per bay per month, converted at 22 ZAR to 1 GBP, being £1,488 per month.
  • Consumables (stationery, food, beverages) billed at cost plus 10%.
  • Taxes. All charges exclude VAT and other taxes at the prevailing rate.
Full agreement. Grant of occupation, breach, confidentiality, liability, insurance, governing law (South Africa) and the remaining Part A and Part B clauses of the 5 October 2026 MSA continue to apply. This proposal does not replace them.
