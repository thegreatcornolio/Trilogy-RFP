01 — Opportunity

The opportunity

  • South Africa has been a top-2 global BPO destination for five years, with the UK’s top 15 BPO firms already resident and US demand now accelerating.
  • Healthcare is the fastest-growing but least-served vertical: US payers are offshoring member support at scale — Humana alone moved 2,000 agents to Cape Town via EXL and TTEC.
  • Very few South African BPOs can show real healthcare administration, regulatory or compliance capability — Trilogy included, on its own.
  • Medscheme has that healthcare capability but is not a known brand in the UK/US and has limited sales & marketing reach to capture this demand alone.

05 — Competitive advantage

Why this makes strategic sense, and why now

South Africa is a top-2 global BPO destination

The country has held this position for five years running, with the UK’s top 15 BPO firms already resident and US demand now accelerating on the same trajectory.

Healthcare capability is the scarce ingredient

Regulatory complexity kept this vertical offshore-resistant for years — few SA providers can show credible healthcare compliance and administration experience. Medscheme can; most competitors cannot.

Global BPOs are racing to acquire this exact capability

International players are actively investing in local healthcare capability to capture the offshoring wave — the window to move first, rather than follow, is open but narrowing.

08 — Shared Services Model

Why a BPO Centralized Shared Services Hub

Centralising these disciplines does more than tidy an org chart — it changes what Medscheme can measure, govern and scale.

Aligned to international standards

One consistent operating framework, benchmarked against global BPO best practice — not a different standard at every site.

Near real-time analytics

A single data layer across every discipline, surfacing trends and risk as they emerge, not weeks later.

Real-time operating reporting

One live view of operational performance for Medscheme leadership, instead of fragmented, delayed reports.

Commercial contract management

Centralised oversight of SLAs, pricing and commercial terms, keeping governance and accountability in one place.

Why centralise Recruitment, Training, QA & Analytics, WFM, MIS and Onboarding?

✓ Consistency

Every site works to the same standard, not a different version of “good” in each location.

✓ Economies of scale

Specialist recruiters, trainers and QA analysts serve the whole operation, instead of being duplicated per site.

✓ Faster scaling

New teams launch against a proven playbook instead of starting from a blank page.

✓ One source of truth

Performance data feeds a single model, so an improvement in one area is visible everywhere.

08 — Shared Services Model

Discovery

Trilogy practitioners embed within the Medscheme environment to observe and document how each Hub discipline operates today.

Step 02

Evaluation

Each discipline in the Hub is assessed individually against international best practices, process maturity and outcomes.

Step 03

Measurement

Findings are benchmarked against Trilogy’s best-practice standards, surfacing the specific gaps and opportunities.

Step 04

Improvement

A tailored recommendation sets out the shared services model best suited to Medscheme’s environment and goals.
