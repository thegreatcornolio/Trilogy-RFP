05 — Key terms

Occupation in brief

The commercial schedule in this proposal is the face of the Trilogy CX Master Services Agreement dated 26 August 2026. The points below are the terms that affect price, notice and use.

  • Payment of rental. Paid in full, without set-off, at least five business days before each payment interval.
  • Escalation. Rental and other charges increase 7% each year, linked to Redefine, with at least 30 days’ notice, effective on the anniversary of 1 October 2026.
  • Variable charges. Parking may change from time to time and is invoiced monthly in arrears, due within 30 days of invoice.
  • Late payment. Interest at Prime plus 2%; access may be suspended; the agreement may be terminated if unpaid ten business days after written notice.
  • Notice. Either party may terminate for no cause on three calendar months’ written notice, not to be given before the first anniversary, and only once Minimum Commitment obligations have been fulfilled.
  • Hours. Ability to operate 24/7 in this location.
  • Cleaning and security are included via the Cleaning & Security Fee. Back office HR, payroll, facilities and management support is included only if that fee is elected.
  • Parking is excluded from rental: 20 bays at R1,637.10 excl. VAT per bay per month, converted at 22 ZAR to 1 GBP, being £1,488 per month.
  • Consumables (stationery, food, beverages) billed at cost plus 10%.
  • Taxes. All charges exclude VAT and other taxes at the prevailing rate.
Full agreement. Grant of occupation, breach, confidentiality, liability, insurance, governing law (South Africa) and the remaining Part A and Part B clauses of the 26 August 2026 MSA continue to apply. This proposal does not replace them.
